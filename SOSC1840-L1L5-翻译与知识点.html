一、社会为什么需要法律（三大功能）

  1. 维持社会秩序social order、保护社会行动者的合法权利legitimate rights；
  2. 提供稳定性和可预测性stability and predictability；
  3. 调解与解决社会纠纷。

反例：执法不力ineffective law enforcement带来假货fake stuff、盗版pirate stuff、有毒食品poisonous food等问题。

二、正式渠道 vs 非正式渠道formal（全课主轴）

非正式渠道基于自愿合作voluntary cooperation或协商，适用于两种情形：① 国家权力state power（如法院）缺位——「无需法律的秩序order without law」；② 人们不愿诉诸正式法律机构。

「无需法律的秩序」为什么成立（小共同体/村庄中）：个人声誉reputation重要；难以退出exit，以牙还牙tit-for-tat（tit-for-tat）可行。

非正式机制的局限：① 情境依赖——只适合小群体、熟人、面对面社会，对陌生人strangers社会（以产品为媒介的交往）和大规模流动社会失效；② 成本低但不总有约束力binding；③ 对外人未必合理。

正式法律机构的四个条件：① 稳定、清晰、合法的法律；② 独立、廉洁、有能力的法律机构与人员；③ 能限制公权与私权的政治体制（制衡check and balance、选举elections、媒体media、社会运动social movements）；④ 有利于公正执法fair law enforcement的政治社会文化（需长期养成）。

正式法律系统的局限：需要知识、需要钱、过程漫长（「迟到的正义是非正义Justice delayed is justice denied」）、可能太僵化（如农民工王斌余Wang Binyu案）、也可能太宽松（反复轻罪）。

民间顺口溜：大错不犯、小错不断、难死法院、气死公安。

三、传统中国社会为什么不兴诉讼

1. 儒家思想Confucian ideology：强调道德修养the cultivation of moral goodness；成文法formally enacted law的出现是道德衰败moral decline的标志；纠纷扰乱社会与自然秩序，应避免；和解reconciliation优于对簿公堂direct confrontation (in the court)。三大禁忌the three taboos（three taboos）：与殡葬（死亡）、当铺（负债）、打官司（丢脸a loss of face——说明对方不尊重你）沾边。

2. 政府态度（以康熙the Kang Xi Emperor 1662–1723 为例）：不愿民众勤于诉讼——若百姓不怕官府且信任官府，诉讼会多到可怕；告状者应被无情对待使其厌讼、见官发抖；纠纷应「如兄弟般」友好解决；对顽固好讼者，就让他们在法庭上倾家荡产。

后果——庭审是羞辱性的：当事人和证人witnesses须跪着回话；刑讯torture取证；总要有人为「扰乱自然秩序」负责（被告有罪则罚被告，无罪则罚诬告unjustified accusation者）；监狱恶劣（Bird 1982：人间地狱）；腐败严重。因此调解更常用。

Lecture 1 讲义全文翻译（中英对照）

Lecture 1 Introduction to Law and Society in China. Why do we need law in society? a. Maintaining social order and protecting the legitimate rights of social actors. b. Providing stability and predictability. c. Mediating and resolving social disputes. Example: Ineffective law enforcement and the problems in China (fake stuff, pirate stuff, poisonous food).

第一讲：中国法律与社会导论。社会为什么需要法律？
① 维持社会秩序并保护社会行动者的合法权利；
② 提供稳定性和可预测性；
③ 调解与解决社会纠纷。反面例子：中国执法不力的问题（假货、盗版、有毒食品）。

Order can be achieved, and conflict can be resolved through both formal and informal channels. Informal channels of resolving conflicts among citizens or social actors are normally based on their voluntary cooperation or negotiation. Two situations in which informal channels are used: a. When the state power (e.g., the court) is absent (i.e., order without law). b. When the people are reluctant to resort to formal legal institutions.

秩序与纠纷解决可以走正式和非正式两类渠道。非正式渠道通常基于公民或社会行动者之间的自愿合作与协商。两种适用情形：
① 国家权力（如法院）缺位时——「无需法律的秩序」；
② 人们不愿求助正式法律机构时。

Why does "order without law" work? In a relatively small community (i.e., village): a. The reputation of an individual is important to his status in the community. b. It is difficult to exit, and tit-for-tat (以牙还牙) is possible.

「无需法律的秩序」为什么能运转？在相对较小的共同体（如村庄）中：
① 个人声誉对其在社区中的地位至关重要；
② 难以退出，且「以牙还牙」的报复可行。

Limitations of informal mechanism or norms: (1) Context-specific — effective for small groups, families, face-to-face contact, villages, tribal life; not good enough for relationships among strangers who "meet" only in the form of a product; do not work for many problems in large, complex, mobile societies. (2) It is less costly but may not always be binding. (3) It may not be reasonable to others.

非正式机制/规范的局限：
① 情境依赖——对小群体、家庭、面对面接触的村庄与部落生活有效，但对仅以产品为媒介「相遇」的陌生人关系不够用，对大型复杂流动社会的许多问题无能为力；
② 成本较低但未必总有约束力；
③ 对外人而言未必合理。

Formal legal institutions. Conditions: a. Stable, clear, and legitimate laws; b. Independent legal institutions staffed with capable and incorrupt agents — trained, disciplined, with integrity; c. The political system that limits the power of both public and private actors — check and balance, elections, media, social movements (protests); d. Political and social culture conducive to fair law enforcement (it takes a long time for such cultures to emerge).

正式法律机构。成立的四个条件：
① 稳定、清晰、具有合法性的法律；
② 独立的法律机构，人员有能力且廉洁——受过训练、守纪律、有操守；
③ 能同时约束公权力与私人行动者的政治体制——制衡、选举、媒体、社会运动（抗议）；
④ 有利于公正执法的政治与社会文化（这种文化需要很长时间才能形成）。

Limitations of formal legal systems: a. It requires knowledge; b. It requires money; c. It can be a lengthy process — Justice delayed is justice denied (迟到的正义是非正义); d. It can be too rigid (e.g., peasant migrant worker Wang Binyu); e. It can be too relaxed (e.g., repeated less serious crimes). In China, some people: 大错不犯 / 小错不断 / 难死法院 / 气死公安.

正式法律系统的局限：
① 需要知识；
② 需要钱；
③ 过程可能漫长——迟到的正义即非正义；
④ 可能太僵化（如农民工王斌余案）；
⑤ 可能太宽松（如对反复轻罪无可奈何）。中国有些人：大错不犯、小错不断、难死法院、气死公安。

Law and Society in Traditional Chinese Society. Formal judicial process was not promoted. 1. Confucian ideology: a. It emphasizes the cultivation of moral goodness; b. the emergence of formally enacted law was an indication of moral decline; c. disputes mean a disturbance of social and the natural order, and should be avoided if possible; d. reconciliation takes precedence over direct confrontation. One of the three taboos: the undertaker, pawnshop, taking to the court (a loss of face because you were not respected by the offending party).

传统中国社会的法律与社会。正式司法程序不受推崇。
① 儒家思想：强调道德修养；成文法的出现被视为道德衰败的标志；纠纷是对社会与自然秩序的扰乱，应尽量避免；和解优先于直接对抗（对簿公堂）。三大禁忌之一：与殡葬从业者（象征死亡）、当铺（象征负债）、打官司（丢脸——说明对方不尊重你）沾边。

2. Government attitudes. The government was reluctant to see people approach the courts too often (e.g., Kang Xi between 1662 and 1723): a. If people were not afraid of the tribunals and if they had confidence in the tribunals, lawsuits would increase to a frightful amount; b. Those who have recourse to the tribunals should be treated without pity so that they shall be disgusted with law, and tremble to appear before a Magistrate; c. People should settle disputes friendly "like brothers"; d. For those who are troublesome, obstinate and quarrelsome, let them be ruined in the law-courts – that is the justice they deserve.


② 政府的态度。政府不愿看到民众勤于告状（以康熙 1662–1723 年间为例）：a. 若百姓不怕衙门且信任衙门，诉讼会多到骇人；b. 告状者应被无情对待，使其厌恶法律、见了官员就发抖；c. 百姓应当「如兄弟般」友好解决纠纷；d. 对那些惹事、固执、好争吵的人，就让他们在法庭上倾家荡产——这是他们应得的「公道」。

As a result: Court trials were humiliating: a. The litigants and the witnesses had to kneel before the presiding magistrate; b. Torture was used to elicit evidence or a confession of guilt; c. Someone had to be responsible for the disturbed natural order: if the accused is judged guilty, s/he would be punished; if not, the punishment would be assigned to the unjustified accuser; d. Jails were horrible ("If crime, vice, despair, suffering, filth, and cruelty can make a hell on earth, this is one." — Bird, 1982); e. Corruption was serious. Mediation was more commonly used.

后果——庭审是羞辱性的：a. 当事人与证人须跪在主审官员面前；b. 用刑讯取证或逼供；c. 总得有人为「被扰乱的秩序」负责——被告有罪则罚被告，无罪则罚诬告的原告；d. 监狱极其恶劣（Bird 1982：「如果犯罪、邪恶、绝望、苦难、污秽与残忍能造出人间地狱，这就是其一」）；e. 腐败严重。因此调解更被常用。

Dispute-mediation institutions in traditional Chinese societies: 1. The gentry (乡绅) — scholars who had passed the Imperial Examinations on the Confucian classics but might not hold office. Characteristics: having property; marital success (no divorce); conformity with socially approved code of conduct (filial, generous); leisure for tea-shop discussions; education, since "so many were completely illiterate". They serve as mediators or quasi-adjudicatory functions. 2. Village leaders — the village headman and other informal leaders who commanded respect. 3. The families and clans — mediating conflicts within the family or the clan; the face issue often prevented the use of lawsuits (家丑不可外扬). 4. Guilds (行会) — an association of tradesmen of artisans belonging to the same trade or craft; they regulated business activities and mediated conflicts.

传统社会的纠纷调解机制：
① 乡绅——通过儒家经典科举考试但未必做官的士人，特征：有财产、婚姻稳定（不离婚）、行为合乎社会规范（孝顺、慷慨）、有闲暇参与茶馆议事、受过教育（当时「文盲众多」）；他们充当调解人或准裁判角色。
② 村庄领袖——村长及其他受村民尊敬的非正式领袖。
③ 家族与宗族——调解家族或宗族内部冲突；面子问题常阻止诉讼（家丑不可外扬）。
④ 行会——同行业商人或工匠的组织，既规范经营活动也调解纠纷。

Maoist society. After socialist China was established in 1949, the party-state controlled the society in an unprecedented way. After 1958, rural people were formed into rural collectives. By the late 1950s, urban people were formed into work units. In the 1950s, formal legal institutions were established: courts (法院), procuracy (检察院), public security bureau (公安局). (1) Laws and legal institutions were used to achieve political and economic goals, such as land reform and nationalization. (2) Cadres were reminded that law must serve policy.

毛泽东时代的社会：1949 年新中国成立后，党和国家以中国历史上空前的程度控制社会。1958 年后农村居民被组织进农村集体，1950 年代末城市居民被组织进单位。1950 年代建立了正式法律机构：法院、检察院、公安局。
① 法律与法律机构被用来实现政治经济目标（如土地改革、国有化）；
② 干部被要求牢记「法律必须服务于政策」。

Socioeconomic arrangements in Maoist China: a planned economy. 1. The collectivization of agricultural production: Production team (生产队) → Brigade (大队) → Commune (人民公社). Implications: a. The rights of peasants as a collective were not protected; b. Freedom of mobility was limited; c. The source of the divide between urban and rural areas emerged; d. The commune system was ineffective. Civil disputes were mostly addressed within the village community, often with the intervention of village cadres and/or senior villagers.

毛时代的社会经济安排：计划经济。
① 农业生产集体化，三级结构：生产队（若干农户）→ 大队（若干生产队）→ 人民公社（若干大队）。集体化的含义：a. 农民作为集体的权利不受保护；b. 迁徙自由受限；c. 城乡分割由此产生；d. 公社制度低效。民事纠纷大多在村庄社区内部解决，通常由村干部和/或村中长者介入。

2. The work unit system in the urban area (单位): a. An overwhelming majority of the urban population worked in the work units in the public sector; b. Welfare benefits, such as housing and pension, were provided by the work units (factories, government agencies, schools); c. Personnel-file system, together with the rationing system, controlled employees (e.g., getting married needed documents provided by the work unit). Implications: a. Conflicts between individuals across work units were not many as they did not interact much; b. Conflicts within a work unit were mostly addressed within the work unit by the leaders.


② 城市单位制：a. 绝大多数城市人口在公有制单位工作；b. 住房、养老金等福利由单位（工厂、机关、学校）提供；c. 人事档案制度与配给制度共同控制员工（结婚都需要单位出具证明）。含义：a. 跨单位的个人冲突不多——因为彼此往来很少；b. 单位内部冲突大多由单位领导在内部处理。

Politics and law: the Cultural Revolution (1966-1976). About 100 million people in China were negatively affected by this political movement. 34,000 officials, including the president of the country, were executed. a. Legal procedures were not strictly followed, and people could be jailed without being defended; b. Government agencies, including the legal departments, were sometimes paralyzed. From 1978 to 1981, the Supreme Court reviewed 1.2 million criminal cases judged during the Cultural Revolution and corrected the rulings of one-fourth of them, affecting 326,000 people.

Civil: work pay (the oral agreement was illegal); criminal: insult/hurt other people. Result: Zhou paid owed salary, transportation, and compensation (mental pressure), 40,000 yuan; sentenced to jail for six months.

河北民刑交叉案例：1997 年康某到周某的理发店打工，口头约定：干满一年得 2,000 元；不满一年则每月倒赔 350 元学费。到 1998 年 5 月，康某干了八个月分文未得，辞职另找工作。周某找到康某索要 2,000 元「学费」，带人把她抓回理发店殴打，并剃了她的头发（头顶和后脑，即「阴阳头」）。本案兼具民事与刑事性质：民事——劳动报酬（口头协议违法）；刑事——暴力侮辱、伤害他人。判决：民事部分周某支付欠薪、交通费和精神补偿共 40,000 元；刑事部分判处有期徒刑六个月。

Types of law in China: (1) Laws made by the NPC or its standing committee; (2) Administrative stipulations made by the State Council; (3) Local stipulations made by the provincial or lower-level people's congress; (4) Autonomous or special stipulations made by the people's congress of an autonomous region.

中国法律的四种类型：
① 全国人大及其常委会制定的法律；
② 国务院制定的行政法规；
③ 省级及以下人大及其常委会制定的地方性法规；
④ 自治区人大制定的自治条例或特殊规定。

Lawsuit acceptance: (1) Cases related to the implementation of central or local policies may not be accepted by the courts, e.g., family planning; (2) Some cases are accepted with the permission of the local leaders, e.g., lawsuits against the local government; (3) Some civil cases that are difficult to handle may also be rejected, e.g., females who marry someone outside the village want to come back.

案件受理的限制：
① 涉及中央或地方政策执行的案件法院可不受理（如计划生育）；
② 有些案件需地方领导批准才受理（如起诉本地政府）；
③ 难以处理的民事案件也可能被拒（如缺乏法律或明确规定的「外嫁女回村」案）。

The case of striking hard (严打) (1983-1984): Background: deteriorating public security in the early 1980s. Targets: "hooligan" gang members; criminals who fled from place to place; murderers, arsonists, bombers, narcotic traffickers, rapists, robbers and serious thieves; traffickers in women and children; other types of criminals including "counter-revolutionary elements". Principles: harsh punishment (quota system in some places). Agencies: public security bureaus, procuracies, courts. Procedures: swift; extensive. Local officials arrested too many small offenders and re-arrested former felons. Consequences: a. Judges felt sick or collapsed, dying from exhaustion; b. death penalty and life imprisonment cases were delegated to the grassroots courts (then recalled due to their incapability); c. criminals increased from 600,000 to 1.2 million; d. the central authority lost some control — the Ministry of Public Security required local officials to show "revolutionary humanitarianism".

严打（1983–1984）：背景是 1980 年代初治安恶化。打击对象：流氓团伙成员；流窜作案者；杀人犯、纵火犯、爆炸犯、毒贩、强奸犯、抢劫犯与重大盗窃犯；拐卖妇女儿童者；其他罪犯（含「反革命分子」）。原则：从重惩处（一些地方实行指标制）。参与机构：公安局、检察院、法院。程序：快、广——地方官员抓了太多轻罪者，还把已服刑者翻旧账重新逮捕。后果：a. 法官连日高强度审判，有人病倒甚至累死；b. 死刑与无期徒刑案件一度下放基层法院（因其能力不足又收回）；c. 讲义口径：犯罪分子从 60 万增至 120 万（课件口径为处理刑案 165 万起、逮捕 177 万、劳教 32 万）；d. 中央一度部分失控——公安部要求地方官员表现「革命人道主义」。

The "Striking-hard cycle": The campaign was launched in 1983, 1990, and 1996. Effect: (1) Each time after a campaign, the crimes would decline for a while and then rebounded. (2) While the 1983 campaign was more effective, the one in 1996 was not very effective.

「严打周期」：运动于 1983、1990、1996 年发动。效果：
① 每次运动后犯罪先下降一段时间，随后反弹；
② 1983 年那次较有效，1996 年效果不佳。

New developments in courts' independence: a. Government direct intervention in courts is more limited than before; b. Judges may use some measures to counterbalance local governments' intervention (e.g., release a case to outside media; report it to higher-level authorities); c. The establishment of six circuit courts in the country; d. The life responsibility system imposed on judges.

法院独立性的新进展：a. 政府对法院的直接干预比以前少；b. 法官会用一些办法反制地方政府的干预（如把案件透露给外地媒体、向上级机关报告）；c. 全国设立六个巡回法庭；d. 对法官实行终身负责制。

The autonomy of legal departments: the case of the police department. Average deaths of the police in China: 1949-1980: 32; 1981-1989: 131; 1990-1995: 395; 1996-2001: 484. In recent 20 years, 6,000 died. Causes: traffic; overworked and sickness; attacked; helping in risk situations. Difficulties: a. Limited manpower — the population/police ratio: U.S. 379:1; U.K. 384:1; Japan 566:1; China 1,100:1; Hong Kong 170:1. Numbers of police: Guangzhou 25,000; Shenzhen 16,000; Shanghai 40,000; Hong Kong 40,000. b. large numbers of migrant people.

以公安部门为例看法律部门的自主性。中国警察年均死亡人数：1949–1980 年 32 人；1981–1989 年 131 人；1990–1995 年 395 人；1996–2001 年 484 人；近 20 年共约 6,000 人殉职。死因：交通事故、过劳与疾病、遇袭、抢险救助。困难：a. 警力不足——警民比：美国 379:1、英国 384:1、日本 566:1、中国 1,100:1、香港 170:1；警力：广州 25,000、深圳 16,000、上海 40,000、香港 40,000；b. 流动人口庞大。

The police's autonomy or abuse of power: (1) Decisions on accepting cases — cases regarded as "not important" or too difficult may not be accepted; (2) detention — the procuracies corrected illegal detention for more than 380,180 people between 1998 and 2002; (3) issues in questioning suspects; (4) issues in obtaining confessions — evidence obtained through torture may be rejected by the court, but there are issues in questioning.

公安的裁量权与滥权：
① 立案裁量——被认为「不重要」或太难破的案件可能不受理；
② 拘留——1998–2002 年间检察机关纠正非法拘留超过 380,180 人；
③ 讯问嫌疑人的问题；
④ 逼取口供的问题——刑讯取得的证据法院可以排除，但讯问环节仍有问题。

The situation has been improved, from 1999: (1) If there is a death due to torture, the people involved will be punished; the head of the department and the head of the discipline inspection committee need to report the case to the provincial agency in charge of legal affairs; (2) If there are two such cases in a province, the head needs to report to the central agency. Use of new technologies to investigate cases: video camera; face recognition.

1999 年起的改进：
① 刑讯致人死亡的，当事人受罚，部门负责人与纪委负责人须向省级政法主管机关报告；
② 一省发生两起的，须报中央政法主管机关。办案新技术：视频监控、人脸识别。

Week 3 Legal Agents in China. Legal workers in China: Judges: 120,000 to 130,000; Public prosecutors: 69,000; Budget-covered personnel: 180,000; Police officers: 2 million; Auxiliary Police Officers (辅警): 1.4-2 million. Public prosecutors 1998 vs 2011: 112,000 → 151,000; Approved arrests 422,000 → 923,000; Cases filed with courts 262,000 → 824,000.

第三讲：中国的法律工作者。规模：法官 12–13 万；检察官 6.9 万；预算内人员 18 万；警察约 200 万；辅警 140–200 万。检察业务 1998 → 2011：检察官 11.2 万 → 15.1 万；批准逮捕 42.2 万 → 92.3 万；起诉到法院 26.2 万 → 82.4 万件。

Criteria for a judge: "will not be diverted from their duty by any extraneous influences; nor by any hope of reward nor by the fear of penalties; nor by flattering praise nor by indignant reproach." Judges in China: In the early 1980s, about 1 to 2 percent had undergone formal legal education. Backgrounds of judges in the early period: cadres; demobilized soldiers; college graduates; others.

法官的标准：「不为任何外力所动其职守；不为利禄之望、刑罚之惧所动；不为奉承之辞、义愤之责所动。」中国的法官：1980 年代初仅约 1–2% 受过正规法律教育。早期法官来源：干部、转业军人、大学毕业生、其他。

The judge-doctor debate: Should the recruitment of judges be as strict as the recruitment of doctors? (1) High standards for recruiting judges; (2) Knowledge about the law is not the most important. Improvement: In 2001, a unified examination system was introduced — those who work as lawyers, public procurators, and judges must pass a standard test. In 2002, 310,000 people participated in the examination, and 8 percent passed.

法官—医生之争：法官的录用是否应像医生一样严格？一方主张高标准录用；另一方认为法律知识不是最重要的。改进：2001 年引入统一司法考试——律师、检察官、法官须通过同一考试。2002 年 31 万人报考，通过率仅 8%。

Discretion of judges: a. Most lawsuits concern civil and economic disputes, and the party or government intervention is limited; b. Laws grant judges discretion in making rulings; c. Some judges are not satisfied with their income. Forms of corruption: (1) accepting money; (2) accepting other things (e.g., computers); (3) having litigants pay bills; (4) using litigants' property (e.g., cars). Police as individuals: (1) Using state power for personal interests; (2) A sense of privileged citizens.

法官的裁量权：a. 多数诉讼是民事经济纠纷，党政干预有限；b. 法律赋予法官裁判的裁量空间；c. 部分法官对收入不满。腐败形式：收钱；收物（如电脑）；让当事人买单；占用当事人财物（如汽车）。警察个人层面：以国家权力谋私利；自视为特权公民。

Networks and legal cases: Personal connections or social networks are an important factor in China. networks or guanxi (关系): family ties, kinships, friends, classmates, colleagues, being born or raised in the same place. The influence in the legal system: a. direct connections between litigants and legal workers (e.g., judges); b. connections with people who have connections with legal workers; c. building connections through the introduction of a third party (and bribing). How do networks affect justice? (1) When one party in a dispute is connected with judges, but the other is not; (2) When the check mechanisms are weak.

关系与案件：人脉关系（guanxi）是中国人际互动的重要因素——亲属、血缘、朋友、同学、同事、同乡。关系影响司法的三条路径：a. 当事人与法律工作者（如法官）直接认识；b. 认识「与法律工作者有关系的人」；c. 经第三方介绍建立关系（并行贿）。关系何时影响正义：一方有关系而另一方没有时；制约机制薄弱时。

Factors Affecting the Chance of Winning a Lawsuit (%): Facts 12.3; Pertinent laws 2.6; Facts and pertinent laws 8.2; Personal connections 1.5; All the above 75.0; Miscellaneous 0.4. Number of answers 268.

影响胜诉的因素调查（268 份回答）：仅靠事实 12.3%；仅靠相关法律 2.6%；事实+法律 8.2%；仅靠关系 1.5%；以上全部因素 75.0%；其他 0.4%。

Prison management: China enacted the Law on Prison in 1994. Conditions for a reduction of sentence: preventing others' serious crimes; providing reliable tips on serious crimes in the jail; helping legal departments arrest important suspects; having important inventions or technological innovations; saving others' lives in production or daily lives; performing very well during natural disasters or accidents; making other important contributions. Major areas of corruption in prisons: reducing a sentence (减刑); released on parole (假释); released on bail for medical treatment (保外就医).

监狱管理：1994 年颁布《监狱法》。减刑条件：阻止他人重大犯罪；提供狱内重大犯罪的可靠线索；协助司法机关抓捕重要嫌犯；有重要发明或技术革新；在生产生活中救人；在自然灾害或事故中表现突出；有其他重大贡献。监狱腐败的三大环节：减刑、假释、保外就医。

Conditions: (1) Reduced sentence: behave oneself and do something good; starting time requirement: original sentence <5 years → >1 year; 5–10 years → >1.5 years; >10 years → >2 years; life → >2 years; death penalty with a reprieve (死缓) → >3 years. (2) Parole: felons (murder, explosion, robbery, rape, kidnap; sentenced to 10 years or life) cannot be paroled; those with a limited sentence need to finish half; lifetime imprisonment needs 10 years. (3) For medical treatment: those sentenced not for lifetime with serious illness; not applicable to dangerous people or those who want to disable themselves; need documents from hospitals designated by the provincial government.

具体条件：
① 减刑——遵守监规并立功；起始时限：原判不满 5 年须服刑 1 年以上；5–10 年须 1.5 年以上；10 年以上须 2 年以上；无期须 2 年以上；死缓须 3 年以上。
② 假释——重罪犯（杀人、爆炸、抢劫、强奸、绑架，判 10 年以上或无期）不得假释；有期徒刑须服满一半；无期须满 10 年。
③ 保外就医——非无期徒刑且患严重疾病者；危险分子和意图自残者不适用；须省政府指定医院出具证明。

Organized crimes: about one million people engaged in China. Structure: an analysis of 55 criminal organizations in 1993 finds 3.6 percent of the ring leaders did not engage in criminal activities directly; an analysis of 33 organizations in 1997 finds 36.3 percent of the leaders did not engage directly. Forms: (1) family-based; (2) personal connection-based (friends, school mates, fellow villagers); (3) interest-based gangs; (4) ex-prisoners; (5) independent but linked organizations (e.g., smuggling); (6) cross-border organizations. Lecture 6: Suing government agencies in China

第六讲：在中国起诉政府机构

Modes of Resolving State-Citizen Disputes in China: Law 27.1% (Urban 32.9%, Rural 21.5%); Approaching upper-level leaders 26.8%; No action 26.0% (Rural 39.5%); Approaching leaders of the agency concerned 18.3%; Collective petition 13.4%; Approaching the media 4.6%. Total cases 261.

国家与公民间纠纷的解决方式（总样本 261 件，城市 140、农村 121）：诉诸法律 27.1%（城市 32.9%、农村 21.5%）；找上级领导 26.8%；不采取行动 26.0%（农村 39.5%）；找涉事机构的领导 18.3%；集体上访 13.4%；找媒体 4.6%；其他 3.8%。

Suing the government has not been common in China: (1) Work unit system; (2) Rural collective; (3) Before the 1990s there was no law specifying how to sue the government; (4) Traditional influence (屈死不告官).

过去起诉政府并不常见的原因：(1) 单位制使民众与政府直接接触有限；(2) 农村集体的缓冲作用；(3) 1990 年代之前没有法律规定如何起诉政府；(4) 传统观念影响——「屈死不告官」。

New environment and the violation of citizen rights: more direct contact between citizens and government agencies; the making of laws — Administrative Litigation Law (1990), State Compensation Law (1994), Administrative Reconsideration Law (1999); the rise of rights awareness.

新环境与公民权利受侵害：公民与政府机构接触更直接，侵权更常见；立法出台——《行政诉讼法》（1990）、《国家赔偿法》（1994）、《行政复议法》（1999）；公民权利意识上升。

How to sue the government: (1) Administrative Litigation Law allows citizens to sue agencies for rights violations (e.g., illegal demolition of your home). (2) State Compensation Law (e.g., illegal detention by police for a week). (3) Administrative Reconsideration Law — appeal without going to court; it is free (e.g., fined 200 yuan where the law says 50 — apply to the next higher-level police department).

如何起诉政府或其机构：(1)《行政诉讼法》允许公民就权利受侵害起诉政府机构（如政府非法拆你家房子，可向法院起诉）；(2)《国家赔偿法》（如公安局非法拘留你一周，可诉请国家赔偿）；(3)《行政复议法》——不经法院向政府或其上级机关申诉，且免费（如依法只应罚 50 元，派出所却要罚 200 元，可向上一级公安机关即市局申请行政复议）。

The use of lawsuits: administrative litigations increased from 13,000 in 1990 to 100,900 in 2001; tens of national ministries have been sued; state compensation lawsuits reached over 2,000 annually in the early 2000s. All kinds of citizens use lawsuits; lower-level governments, especially at the county level, are most frequently sued.

诉讼的使用情况：行政诉讼案件从 1990 年的 1.3 万件增至 2001 年的 10.09 万件；国家级部委有数十个被起诉过；国家赔偿案在 2000 年代初达每年 2,000 多件。各类公民和社会主体都在使用诉讼；县级及以下基层政府及其机构最常被起诉。

Government/agencies sued in 2012: Township 5.35%; County government 15.94%; County government agencies 39.90%; City government 3.61%; City government agencies 24.31%; Provincial government 4.66%; Provincial agencies 0.67%; Others 5.56%. Total 74,242 cases. Police bureaus, land management departments, and city construction agencies are most frequently sued.

2012 年被诉政府/机构分布（共 74,242 件）：乡镇 5.35%、县政府 15.94%、县政府机构 39.90%、市政府 3.61%、市政府机构 24.31%、省政府 4.66%、省政府机构 0.67%、其他 5.56%。公安局、土地管理部门、城市建设主管部门被诉最多。

Modes of settlement 2012 (procedure-wise): withdrawal 撤诉 28,058 (70.17%); rejected 驳回起诉 7,098 (17.75%); transferred 移送 1,294 (3.24%); closed 终结 199 (0.50%); others 3,182 (7.96%); mediation 赔偿调解 153 (0.38%). Total 39,984.

2012 年结案方式（按程序）（共 39,984 件）：撤诉 28,058 件（70.17%）、驳回起诉 7,098 件（17.75%）、移送 1,294 件（3.24%）、终结 199 件（0.50%）、其他 3,182 件（7.96%）、赔偿调解 153 件（0.38%）。撤诉非常常见。

Forwarded cases: when court A finds it lacks jurisdiction, it forwards the case to the court with jurisdiction; if court B does not believe it has jurisdiction, it can only apply to the upper-level court for designation — it cannot forward the case to another court on its own.

移送管辖：A 法院审查立案申请时发现无管辖权，将案件移送有管辖权的法院；受移送的 B 法院若认为自己也无管辖权，只能报请上级法院指定管辖，不得再自行移送其他法院。

The settlement of administrative litigations: (1) uphold the decision of the government; (2) cancel or partially cancel the specific administrative act; require the defendant to perform his duty; (3) refused.

行政诉讼的裁判方式：(1) 维持政府或其机构的决定；(2) 撤销或部分撤销具体行政行为；责令被告履行法定职责；(3) 驳回。

Rulings in 2012: upheld 维持 16,183 (51.40%); appeal rejected 驳回诉讼请求 7,887 (25.05%); revoked 撤销 4,478 (14.22%); confirmed not legal 违法或无效 1,208 (3.84%); need to perform 履行法定职责 1,074 (3.41%); confirmed legal 262 (0.83%); compensation 149 (0.47%); changing 变更 135 (0.43%); no compensation 108 (0.34%).

2012 年判决结果：维持 16,183 件（51.40%）、驳回诉讼请求 7,887 件（25.05%）、撤销 4,478 件（14.22%）、确认违法或无效 1,208 件（3.84%）、责令履行法定职责 1,074 件（3.41%）、确认合法有效 262 件（0.83%）、赔偿 149 件（0.47%）、变更 135 件（0.43%）、不予赔偿 108 件（0.34%）。

Behavioral Intention in State-Citizen Disputes: intention to use law 41.3% vs actual action 27.1%; intention for collective petition 2.3% vs action 13.4%; intention of no action 11.8% vs actual no action 26.0%.

官民纠纷中的行为意愿与实际行动对比（意愿样本 10,111 人）：愿用法律者 41.3% 而实际仅 27.1%；愿集体上访者 2.3% 而实际达 13.4%；打算不行动者 11.8% 而实际 26.0%。

Why is withdrawal so commonly used? (1) The government corrects its conduct and avoids court to "save face"; (2) it places pressure on the plaintiffs and forces them to withdraw.

撤诉为何如此常见？(1) 政府或其机构主动纠正行为、为「保面子」避免出庭；(2) 对原告施压、迫使其撤诉。

Why are government agencies sued? (1) Violating the rights of citizens or other social actors; (2) failing to protect the rights of citizens — "inaction".

政府机构为何被诉？(1) 侵犯公民或其他社会主体的权利；(2) 未能保护公民权利，即「不作为」。

Peasants' use of lawsuits (Shanxi, N=745): suing the government is still not common — approaching leaders 51.1%, personal connections 20.8%, lawyers or courts 9.9%. But rights awareness is on the rise. Reasons for lawsuits: financial burdens, village elections, land use, others.

农民对诉讼的使用（山西调查，N=745）：起诉政府仍不常见——与国家主体发生纠纷时找相关领导 51.1%、私人关系调解 20.8%、找律师或法院仅 9.9%。但农民的权利意识在上升。诉讼原因：农民负担、村委会选举、土地使用等。

Problems with administrative litigations: local officials' ignorance of legal obligation (seldom appear in court); litigation requires knowledge, courage, and political wisdom; administrative litigations have never exceeded 2% of all lawsuits. It is difficult to file a case (立案), make a ruling (审理), and enforce a case (执行).

行政诉讼存在的问题：地方官员缺乏法律义务意识（很少出庭）；诉讼需要知识、勇气和政治智慧；行政诉讼从未超过法院受案总数的 2%。存在「三难」：立案难、审理难、执行难。

In 2014, the Chinese authority began amending the Administrative Litigation Law, which took effect in May 2015, aiming to address the three difficulties. Major amendments: 1. scope from 8 to 10 items; 2. state agencies not allowed to prevent courts from accepting cases; 3. lawsuits can be orally presented; 4. filing period extended from 3 to 6 months; 5. leaders of the sued agency should appear in court; 6. a case can be tried outside the place where the agency is located; 7. leaders can be detained if the agency refuses to enforce a ruling. The case-filing examination system (立案审查制) was changed to the registration system (立案登记制). In May 2015, courts accepted 26,000 cases — an increase of 221%.

2015 年《行政诉讼法》修改：2014 年启动修法，2015 年 5 月生效，旨在解决「三难」。主要修订：
① 受案范围从 8 项扩至 10 项；
② 行政机关不得干预法院受理案件；
③ 可以口头起诉；
④ 起诉期限从 3 个月延长到 6 个月；
⑤ 被诉机关负责人应当出庭；
⑥ 案件可在行政机关所在地之外审理；
⑦ 拒不执行判决的，机关负责人可被拘留。立案审查制改为立案登记制——2015 年 5 月法院受案 2.6 万件，同比增长 221%。

Increasing pressure on administrative review agencies — maintaining government agencies' decisions: review agencies 55.8%–59.7% vs courts 20.2%–22.5% (2011–2014). If the applicant refuses the review outcome and sues, the reviewing agency is the defendant; if the reviewing agency (A) maintained the original agency's (B) decision, both A and B are defendants.

行政复议机关压力增大：维持原决定的比例，复议机关 55.8%–59.7%，远高于法院的 20.2%–22.5%（2011–2014 年）。申请人不服复议结果而起诉的，复议机关为被告；若复议机关（A）维持了原机关（B）的决定，则 A 和 B 为共同被告。

State Compensation: from 1995 to 2004, courts accepted 15,867 cases; compensation was made in 5,442. Yearly: 1995: 154 (64 compensated, 42%); 1996: 110 (40%); 1998–2002: 11,321 (35%); 2003–2007: 13,000; 2008–2012: 8,684; 2013–2016: 16,889.

国家赔偿：1995–2004 年法院共受理 15,867 件，其中 5,442 件获得赔偿。历年数据：1995 年 154 件（赔偿 64 件，42%）；1996 年 110 件（40%）；1998–2002 年 11,321 件（35%）；2003–2007 年 13,000 件；2008–2012 年 8,684 件；2013–2016 年 16,889 件。

The scope of state compensation — in criminal cases: mistaken detention, mistaken arrest, mistaken judgment, torture or hurt during questioning, inaction, wounded or killed by police's illegal use of weapons. Other situations: illegal freezing of property; fine paid or property confiscated but later found not guilty.

国家赔偿的范围——刑事案件中：错误拘留、错误逮捕、错误判决、讯问时遭刑讯或其他伤害、不作为、因警察违法使用武器或器械致伤致死。其他情形：违法冻结财产；已缴罚款或被没收财产后被判无罪。

Case: A peasant in Hubei was detained as a murder suspect in 1989; in 1999 the court ruled him not guilty. He was illegally detained for 3,879 days and received 191,932 yuan. But the law does not apply to cases closed before it took effect (1 January 1995): in 1977 a peasant was sentenced to seven years; the verdict was revoked in 1996, but his compensation application was rejected by both county and intermediate courts.

案例：湖北一农民 1989 年被作为杀人嫌疑犯拘留，1999 年法院判其无罪释放，被非法羁押 3,879 天，获国家赔偿 191,932 元。但赔偿法不溯及 1995 年 1 月 1 日生效前已结的旧案：一农民 1977 年因贪污被判 7 年，1996 年县法院撤销原判，其赔偿申请被县法院和中级法院先后驳回。

New developments: the law was amended in 2012 (effective 2013): detained but the case not pursued; executed cases later proven innocent; citizens injured during law enforcement or questioning; compensation for mental suffering (精神损失); payment within 22 days. Remaining issues: scope still limited; compensation still too low, based on the national average daily pay of the previous year — raised to 495.94 yuan/day in 2026.

新进展：2012 年修法（2013 年生效）：被拘留后案件不再追诉、已执行后证明无罪、执法或讯问过程中受伤，均可获赔；新增精神损害赔偿；赔偿款应在 22 天内支付。遗留问题：赔偿范围仍有限；标准仍偏低，按上年度全国职工日均工资计算——2026 年提高到每日 495.94 元。

The abolition of labor camp (劳动教养): detention without going through the court, based on police regulations; used for less serious crimes or political crimes. Beginning in 1956 for political enemies; 1957 State Council decision; before 1979 no maximum period — some detained as long as 20 years; in 1979, 1–3 years, extendable by one more year. People in labor camps: 1955: 10,000; 1983: 220,000; 2003: 310,000; 2012: 60,000. The system was abolished in 2013.

劳动教养的废除：依公安部门规章将人关入劳教所、剥夺自由，无需经过法院，常用于处理轻罪或政治犯。始于 1956 年（安置政治敌人）；1957 年国务院决定针对不务正业、违法乱纪者；后扩大到「不够逮捕条件、但放出会加剧失业」的人。1979 年前无最长期限，有人被关长达 20 年；1979 年规定 1–3 年、必要时可延长 1 年。在教人数：1955 年 1 万、1983 年 22 万、2003 年 31 万、2012 年 6 万。该制度于 2013 年废止。

一、人们为什么守法to obey the law / 不守法

守法原因：① 制裁sanctions的作用（可信的惩罚credible punishment）；② 同伴群体、良心conscience、道德感召moral appeal、尴尬与羞耻（环境）；③ 合法性legitimacy与对权威authority的普遍尊重。

Lecture 7: Law enforcement in China. Topics: reasons for observing the law; the situation of law enforcement in China; reasons for the difficulties in enforcing court rulings; some measures.

第七讲：中国的执法。议题：守法的原因；中国执法的状况；法院判决执行难的原因；应对措施。

Why do people obey the law? a. The role of sanctions (credible punishment); b. the role of peer groups, conscience, moral appeal, embarrassment, and shame (environment); c. the role of legitimacy and general respect for authority. Why do people not obey the law? (1) Disputable laws; (2) the environment; (3) limited risks or costs.

人们为什么守法？
① 制裁的作用（可信的惩罚）；
② 同伴群体、良心、道德感召、尴尬与羞耻的作用（环境）；
③ 合法性与对权威的普遍尊重。为什么不守法？
① 法律有争议；
② 环境；
③ 违法风险或成本有限。

Disputable laws: the case of city management (城管). It is a government unit responsible for managing various issues regarding city operation: city appearance, environment and hygiene; urban planning, transportation, public facilities, water supply, parking, city forestry, construction sites, city rivers, unlicensed taxis, unlicensed tour guides, unlicensed business, etc.

争议法律的案例：城管。城管是负责管理城市运行各类事务的政府单位，职责涵盖市容环境卫生、城市规划、交通、城市公共设施、供水、停车、城市绿化、工地管理、河道管理、无证出租车、无证导游、无证经营等。

History: In 1996 the State Council issued a directive proposing concentration of power in managing city matters. In 1997 the first agency was established in Beijing, then extended nationwide. By 2006, the State Council had approved it in 79 cities in 23 provinces, and local governments approved it in more than 200 cities. China has a quota of 450,000 city management staff, 300,000 of them college graduates; the actual number could be over one million.

城管的历史：1996 年国务院发文提出集中城市管理权力；1997 年第一个城管机构在北京成立，随后推广全国；到 2006 年，国务院已批准 23 个省的 79 个城市设立，地方政府自行批准的超过 200 个城市。全国城管编制 45 万人，其中 30 万为大学毕业生，实际人数可能超过 100 万。

Problems faced by the agency: personnel quota and social security; heavy workload; health and risk; personal honor and image. Why is it tied to "violent law enforcement"? 1. Ineffective management of staff; 2. pressure of performance assessment; 3. violent resistance to law enforcement is increasing.

城管面临的问题：编制与社保、工作量大、健康与风险、个人荣誉与形象。为何与「暴力执法」挂钩？
① 对人员管理不善；
② 绩效考核压力——管理不力影响考核；
③ 暴力抗法事件在增多。

Human trafficking: trafficked population is between 6 and 8 million, 1.2 million of them children; cross-border: 600,000 to 800,000.

人口贩卖：被贩人口约 600–800 万，其中 120 万是儿童；跨境贩卖 60–80 万。

Three major modes of child trafficking: M1. Abduction and sale — by abduction, violence, coercion, deception, abuse of power, etc. M2. Parental sale — initiated by parents who find middlemen or buyers themselves. M3. Organized trafficking — parents induced or recruited by middlemen; deals set before or after birth; the middleman is part of a trafficking network. Modes 2013–2016: M1 31.8%, M2 28.7%, M3 39.5% (total 689 cases).

儿童贩卖的三种主要模式：M1 拐骗贩卖——以拐骗、暴力、胁迫、欺骗、滥用职权等手段实施；M2 父母出卖——父母主动发起并自行寻找中间人或买家；M3 有组织贩卖——父母被中间人引诱或招募，孩子出生前后即达成交易，中间人属于贩卖网络。2013–2016 年模式分布（共 689 例）：M1 占 31.8%、M2 占 28.7%、M3 占 39.5%（广东 M3 45.7%、云南 M3 60.2%、山东 M1 36%）。

Who was targeted? Age distribution of trafficked women (n=103): 11–13: 16%; 14–17: 51%; 18–20: 16%; 21–25: 11%. How were the women acquired? Introducing jobs 91%; made unconscious with medicine / kidnapped 9%.

受害者是谁？被拐妇女年龄分布（n=103）：11–13 岁 16%、14–17 岁 51%、18–20 岁 16%、21–25 岁 11%。如何被拐？91% 以介绍工作为名被骗；9% 被下药迷晕或绑架。

Who are involved? Direct relationships (parents, husbands, sons, siblings) 17%; close relationships (relatives, boyfriends, friends, neighbors/fellow villagers) 64%; others (internet "friends", employers, job centers, fake policemen) 15%. Total 69.

谁在参与贩卖？（n=69）直接关系（父母、丈夫、儿子、兄弟姐妹）17%；亲密关系（亲属、男友、朋友、邻居/同乡）64%；其他（网友、雇主、职介中心、假警察）15%。

Explaining population trafficking: (1) Social environment — trusting people who cannot be trusted; (2) poor law enforcement (punishment not credible): local officials lack incentive to prevent it; the police lack resources (manpower, money) and a strong sense of responsibility.

对人口贩卖的解释：
① 社会环境——轻信了不该信任的人；
② 政府执法不力（惩罚不可信）：地方官员没有预防的积极性；警力资源不足（人手、经费）；警察责任心不强。

Enforcement of court rulings: before 1986, 70% of debtors followed rulings voluntarily; by 1996 it declined to less than 30%. In 2005, courts received 2.05 million applications for enforcement. Limited compensation: since 2001 about 4 million criminal cases annually, 40–50% solved; 80% of victims or families were not compensated — about 3.2 million cases.

法院判决的执行：1986 年前约 70% 的被执行人自觉履行，到 1996 年降到不足 30%；2005 年法院收到 205 万件执行申请。赔偿不足：2001 年以来每年刑事立案约 400 万件，破案率 40–50%；据最高检察院统计，80% 的受害人或其家属未获赔偿——约 320 万件未获赔偿。

Types of cases difficult to enforce: traffic accidents (large amounts; hard to find debtors and property); private lending; traditionally difficult cases — personal injury, employees injured, civil suits collateral to criminal proceedings (刑事附带民事诉讼); females married to outsiders (外嫁女); others.

难执行的案件类型：
① 交通事故（金额大、被执行人和财产难找）；
② 民间借贷；
③ 传统难案——人身损害赔偿纠纷、雇员受害赔偿、刑事附带民事诉讼；
④ 外嫁女案件；
⑤ 其他。

Causes of difficult enforcement: no property 51.15%; ownership to be defined 1.62%; intervention of local government agencies 1.27%; upper-level court postponement 1.18%; upper-level court mediation 1.24%; miscellaneous 46.54%.

执行难的原因（统计）：无财产 51.15%；财产权属待定 1.62%；地方政府机构干预 1.27%；上级法院指令暂缓 1.18%；上级法院调解 1.24%；其他 46.54%。

Debtors evade responsibility: hiding from court people; non-confrontational resistance; using divorce to transfer property — Article 41 of the Marriage Law: common debt is the common responsibility of both parties; if common property is insufficient, the parties should agree on payment, or the court decides. "Fake" divorce: one spouse accepts all debts and leaves property to the other in the name of raising the kid and parents; or they do not report debts at divorce so creditors cannot locate the responsible person. Also: giving property away before the ruling; violence; guanxi.

被执行人逃避责任的方式：躲避法院人员；非对抗性抗拒；利用离婚转移财产逃债——《婚姻法》第 41 条规定：离婚时共同债务由双方共同偿还；共同财产不足清偿或财产归各自所有的，由双方协议清偿，协议不成由法院判决。「假离婚」手法：一方以抚养孩子、赡养父母为名揽下全部债务，把全部或大部分财产留给另一方；或离婚时不申报债务，使债权人找不到责任人。此外还有：判决前把财产赠与他人；暴力抗拒；动用关系。

Other causes: state compensation is not included in the government budget; legal departments are not active; intervention by government or its agencies; problems with the law.

其他原因：国家赔偿未纳入政府预算；司法部门不积极；政府或其机构干预（如领导打招呼）；法律本身存在问题。

How is a court judgment enforced? The court has an enforcement tribunal (执行庭) accepting litigants' applications. Two opposite phenomena: some courts put their best people in the tribunal; others put the least capable. Reasons reported by lawyers (N=279): insufficient efforts by courts 82.7%; government intervention 45.5%; social networks 44.8%; defendants' resistance 24.4%; loopholes in law 11.5%.

判决如何执行？法院设执行庭受理当事人的执行申请。两个相反现象：有的法院把最优秀的人放进执行庭，有的把能力最差的人放进去。律师反映的执行难原因（N=279，多选）：法院努力不足 82.7%、政府干预 45.5%、社会关系网影响 44.8%、被告抗拒 24.4%、法律漏洞 11.5%。

Problems with existing laws: under the Civil Procedure Law, a litigant should apply for enforcement within 2 years; in some other countries the span is 5 to 20 years.

现行法律的问题：按《民事诉讼法》，当事人应在 2 年内申请执行；而在其他一些国家，期限为 5 到 20 年。

Difficulties in asset realization (Taobao auctions): total 8.67 million auctions worth 22,298 billion yuan; residential housing 37.0% of cases and 25.0% of value; business housing 19.1% of cases and 28.7% of value. Clearance rates: vehicles 57%, jewellery 30%, residential housing 24%, land 19%, commercial 11%, other housing 10%; overall 19%. Discounts around 19%–27%.

资产变现难（淘宝司法拍卖数据）：拍卖共 867 万余件、总价值约 2.23 万亿元；住宅占件数 37.0%、价值 25.0%；商业用房占件数 19.1%、价值 28.7%。成交率：车辆 57%、珠宝 30%、住宅 24%、土地 19%、商业用房 11%、其他住房 10%，总体仅 19%；折价率普遍在 19%–27%。

Courts are weak versus public institutions; weak in enforcing rulings outside their home locations (异地执行) — local protectionism (地方保护主义). Difficulties: locating the defendant's property; getting banks' cooperation; getting support from local legal departments and police.

法院的弱势：面对公权力机构处于弱势；异地执行难——源于地方保护主义。异地执行三难：难以找到被告财产；难获当地银行配合；难获当地司法部门和警方支持。

Local officials enjoy many benefits but are under pressure — assessing performance (survey of 121 city-level officials): performance 52.1%; opportunities 21.5%; personal connections 18.2%.

地方官员：好处多但压力大。对 121 名市级官员的调查（考核中最看重的因素）：政绩 52.1%、机遇 21.5%、关系 18.2%。

Addressing measures: more efforts by central authorities to require local compliance; the help of the media; more efforts by courts / criminal law; the establishment of the social credibility system (社会信用制度). Dishonest debtors in China (老赖).

应对措施：
① 中央加大力度要求地方服从；
② 借助媒体；
③ 法院更多作为、动用刑法；
④ 建立社会信用体系——将失信被执行人（「老赖」）列入名单并公示惩戒。
